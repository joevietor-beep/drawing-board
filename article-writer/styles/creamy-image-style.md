# Creamy.Digital Image Style Guide

## Purpose

Every image should reinforce the article's message by telling a visual story.

Images should communicate optimism, professionalism, and human connection.

---

## Overall Mood

Bright

Optimistic

Professional

Thoughtful

Modern

Inviting

Authentic

---

## Lighting

Prefer:

- Natural daylight
- Bright office lighting
- Morning sunlight
- Coffee shop ambiance
- Warm professional interiors

Avoid dark, gloomy, or overly dramatic lighting.

---

## People

People should feel authentic.

Use:

- Expressive faces
- Natural eyes
- Genuine smiles
- Confident body language
- Diverse ages and backgrounds
- Professional clothing appropriate to the industry

Avoid exaggerated cartoon expressions.

---

## Artistic Direction

Rotate naturally between:

- Editorial Illustration
- Photorealistic
- Pencil Sketch
- Ink Illustration
- Watercolor
- Digital Painting
- Mixed Media
- Realistic scenes enhanced with illustrated elements

The medium should support the article rather than becoming repetitive.

---

## Storytelling

Every image should communicate an idea, not simply decorate the page.

Ask:

"What business story does this image tell?"

---

## Composition

Prefer:

- Landscape orientation
- Cinematic composition
- Layered depth
- Interesting perspectives
- Active scenes
- Human interaction

Avoid flat, centered compositions whenever possible.

---

## Color Palette

Bright.

Modern.

Professional.

Colorful without becoming loud.

Avoid muted terracotta dominance.

Avoid dull corporate palettes.

---

## Never Use

- Tiny dot eyes
- Generic office clip art
- Flat vector people
- Terracotta-heavy palettes
- Boring people pointing at charts
- Repetitive business handshake scenes
- Overused AI clichés

---

## Variety

Every article should feel visually unique.

No two articles should use the same artistic treatment unless the subject truly demands it.

Readers should recognize a Creamy.Digital article by its editorial quality—not by repetitive artwork.

---

## SEO Support

Every generated image should include:

- SEO-friendly filename
- Descriptive title
- Helpful alt text
- Image description

All metadata should naturally relate to the article's topic and keywords.

---

## Visual Storytelling

Images should never simply illustrate a paragraph.

They should communicate the emotional message of the section before the reader begins reading.

Every illustration should answer:

"What story is this image telling?"

The best images communicate confidence, clarity, progress, trust, learning, or transformation.
