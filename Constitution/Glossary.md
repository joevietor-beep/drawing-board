# Workbench Glossary

## Awareness

The practice of becoming present before engaging with the world.

---

## Atelier

The workshop where observations become patterns, patterns become messages, and messages become contributions.

---

## Card

The atomic presentation of an object.

A card answers:

What is here?

What question arises?

What engagement follows?

---

## Commitment

A chosen engagement aligned with the current mission.

---

## Contribution

Something worthy of another person's attention.

---

## Conversation

A relationship event from which observations may arise.

---

## Discernment

The practice of recognizing what is true, useful, timely, and worthy.

---

## Engagement

Thoughtful interaction with people, ideas, or work.

---

## Observation

Something genuinely noticed during engagement.

---

## Observer

The AI companion that notices patterns without replacing human judgment.

---

## Pattern

A recurring relationship among observations.

---

## Stewardship

Faithful care of attention, relationships, knowledge, and contribution.

---

## Workbench

An environment for stewardship, engagement, and contribution.
